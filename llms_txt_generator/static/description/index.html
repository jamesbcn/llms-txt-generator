LLMs.txt Generator for Odoo

Make your Odoo website AI-ready

This free module automatically generates a /llms.txt file following llmstxt.org guidelines — improving your website’s visibility to large language models (LLMs) such as ChatGPT and other AI assistants.

✨ Key Features

  • Automatic generation of /llms.txt at yourdomain.com/llms.txt
  • Includes multilingual URLs for full SEO & AI discoverability
  • Dynamic updates — no manual editing required
  • Fully aligned with the llmstxt.org standard
  • Install directly from your Odoo Apps dashboard — no technical setup

Why it matters

Just like sitemap.xml and robots.txt help websites communicate with search engines, llms.txt helps websites communicate with AI models. By installing this module, you make your Odoo site easier to discover, reference, and understand in the AI era.

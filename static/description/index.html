LLMS TXT Generator for Odoo

Make your Odoo website SEO & LLM-friendly

This module generates an llms.txt file automatically, following the llmstxt.org guidelines. It improves your site’s SEO and makes your content easier for large language models and search engines to understand.

Features

  • Automatic generation of /llms.txt
  • Includes Website Pages, Blog Posts, and Products (if eCommerce installed)
  • Multi-language support
  • SEO-friendly attribution
  • Fully compliant with llmstxt.org specifications
